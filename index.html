Privacy Policy

AtahanHobbySpace — AI & Technology content automation

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our content-automation service (the “Service”) handles data when it creates and publishes short-form videos to a YouTube channel that we own and operate. The Service is a personal, automated publishing pipeline for AI & technology content. By using or interacting with the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

The Service is operated by the owner of the AtahanHobbySpace YouTube channel. For any privacy question or request, contact us at the email address in the “Contact” section below.

2. Information we access

The Service connects to Google and YouTube APIs using the channel owner’s own account. Through this connection it may access:

  • Basic Google account profile information (such as account identifier and email) used only to authenticate the channel owner.
  • YouTube account and channel access needed to upload, manage, and set metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status) for videos on the owner’s own channel.
  • Operational content generated by the pipeline itself — video titles, scripts, descriptions, hashtags, media files, and scheduling data — stored in the owner’s own Google Sheet and processing tools.

The Service does not access, collect, or process personal data belonging to viewers of the channel or any third parties.

3. How we use the information

  • To authenticate with Google/YouTube and upload videos to the owner’s channel.
  • To schedule and track which videos have been produced and published.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the automated publishing workflow.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the owner’s own content.

4. Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

The Service’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google/YouTube APIs is used solely to provide and improve the Service’s publishing functionality and is never sold or transferred to third parties except as required to operate the Service, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share accessed data with third parties. Data may be processed by the infrastructure providers the Service relies on (for example workflow automation, media rendering, and hosting providers) strictly to perform the publishing function, under their respective terms.

6. Data retention and removal

Authentication tokens are retained only while the automation is active. The channel owner can revoke the Service’s access at any time via the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all further access.

7. YouTube

Because the Service uses YouTube API Services, your use of it is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect credentials and tokens, including storing them within secured automation infrastructure and limiting access to the channel owner. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, with the “Last updated” date revised accordingly.
